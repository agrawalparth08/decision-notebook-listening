How to use this series

Each episode does three things. It finds the one thread that holds the subject together. It walks through the frameworks as tools with a job, not definitions to memorise. And it tells the case stories, because that is where the ideas stop being ideas.

Treat the episodes as the friend who explains the case the night before class. They are not a replacement for reading the cases. Your position in every episode is remembered on this device, so you can stop and pick up later.
