Hello, and welcome. This is the Decision Notebook listening library.

Here is the idea. Over the last months, I have worked through more than twenty subjects of a general management programme. Economics, finance, operations, marketing, strategy, people, law, analytics. Each one taught by a professor who has spent years compressing their field into eight sessions.

Now, notes are wonderful. But notes need a desk. This series needs only your ears. It is built for the commute, the walk, the gym, the fifteen minutes before you sleep.

Each subject gets its own full episode, thirty to fifty minutes, long enough to actually teach and not merely summarise. In each episode we do three things. First, we find the one thread that holds the whole subject together, because every subject has one. Second, we walk through the frameworks, not as definitions to memorise but as tools with a job. When do you reach for this one and not that one? That is the question that matters in an exam and in a boardroom. Third, we tell the stories, because the cases are where the ideas stop being ideas. A cookie shop in a dorm room. A steel plant in Gujarat. A banker everyone admired and nobody wanted to work with.

One request before we start. Do not treat these episodes as a replacement for the cases. Treat them as the friend who explains the case to you the night before class, so that when you read it yourself, everything clicks faster.

The style here is simple. We go slow on the hard parts. We repeat the things worth repeating. And we always end with the one sentence you should carry out of the room.

So plug in your earphones. Let us begin with the first subject, and let us see how far the whole thing goes.

This is the Decision Notebook listening library. Made by a student, for students.
